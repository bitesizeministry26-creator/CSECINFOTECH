🧩 Hardware Lab

Explore the major components of a computer system.

🧠

CPU

The Central Processing Unit executes instructions and processes data.

Specs: Clock speed, number of cores, cache.

⚡

RAM

Temporary memory used by programs while they are running.

Specs: Capacity, speed and type.

💾

Storage

Stores data permanently.

Examples: HDD, SSD, NVMe SSD.

🎮

GPU

Processes graphics and visual information. Important for gaming, 3D modelling and video editing.

🔋

Power Supply Unit

Converts electrical power and supplies components with power.

Spec: Wattage.

🧱

Motherboard

The main circuit board that connects the major components.

Contains slots, sockets and interfaces.

❄️

Cooling System

Removes heat generated by components. Examples include fans and liquid cooling.

🖥️

Monitor

An output device used to display visual information.

Specs: Resolution, refresh rate and size.
